In 2006, Zefrank forwarded the concept of [Brain Crack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sHCQWjTrJ8), and I have been using it ever since. In short, brain crack is what ideas turn into when you leave them, un-started, in your brain for too long. The video should be considered a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage item, so just go watch it instead of having me explain it.
